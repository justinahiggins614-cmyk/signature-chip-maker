Welcome to the Chip Maker and Archive

Original Signature-line chip designs — every chip type ever made or projected, marching to 1,000,000. Here’s how to use it:

  1. Find a chip. Describe what you need in the Chip Finder in plain words — or type an exact design ID like JAH-CHIP-000123. It never invents designs.
  2. Browse by family or letter. Open the Browse tab: the 16 family buttons (CPU, GPU, NPU, and more) and the A–Z row filter the design grid. Tap All to clear.
  3. Open a design. Tap any card for the full spec table, the circuit-board drawing, block-by-block breakdown, and fabrication readiness. “✓ Verify design” re-checks the record’s hash in your browser.
  4. Listen and save. Read aloud speaks the spec; Copy and Download save it as .txt / .json. The Chip Helper on every record answers from that design’s own spec sheet.

How to use this site

🔍 Chip Finder

What: the search box at the top. Does: matches your plain words against real archived designs, or opens an exact ID. How: type e.g. “low power sensor chip for a wearable”, or JAH-CHIP-000123. The finder searches the archive only — it never invents designs.

⚙ Families and A–Z

What: the family filter row and the A–Z row on the Browse tab. Does: filters the grid to one chip family (CPU, GPU, NPU, SoC, MCU, FPGA, memory, sensors, power, quantum, photonic, neuromorphic, DSP, modem, security, chiplet) or to names starting with a letter. How: open the Browse tab, tap a family or a letter; tap All to clear.

★ Featured design

What: the gold-bordered spotlight box. Does: shows one real design — spec summary plus the circuit-board drawing — on every visit. How: “Open the full spec →” opens its record; 🎲 Surprise me opens a random design.

📄 Design records

What: the grid of design cards on the Browse tab. Does: every card is one real design: full spec table (purpose, architecture, process node, transistors, die size, package, voltage, clock, power, interfaces), circuit-board drawing, block-by-block breakdown, fabrication readiness. Every record shows its honesty status: CONCEPT / NOT_SIMULATED / NOT_TESTED / NOT_MANUFACTURED. How: open the Browse tab, tap any card; “Load more designs” extends the grid.

✓ Verify design

What: the verify button on every record. Does: recomputes the record’s SHA-256 hash in your browser and compares it to the archive index. How: open any design, tap “✓ Verify design” — ✓ MATCH means byte-identical to the shipped archive.

🔊 Read aloud · ⧉ Copy · ⬇ Downloads

What: the record toolbar. Does: Read aloud speaks the spec (floating bar: Stop / Pause / Resume); Copy spec copies the plain text; downloads save the full record as .txt / .json with statuses. How: the buttons on any open record.

RECORD panel

What: the record action row. Does: ↗ OPEN opens the record’s own link; ⇆ SOURCE shows the design origin; # ID shows and copies the design ID; ⚙ VERSION shows engine and schema version; ⤴ SHARE shares the record link. How: tap the icons on any open record.

🤖 Chip Helper · ⚠ Report

What: the AI helper on every record. Does: answers from that design’s own spec sheet only — process node, power, pins, transistors, clock, materials, board sections, lineage. How: ask it anything about the design; “⚠ Report problem” files a local-only report on your device.

◑ Dark mode · data links

What: the ◑ button top-right and the footer links. Does: toggles an inverted dark theme; footer links expose api.json, chips-catalog.json, the static design index, and the sitemap. How: press ◑; your choice is remembered.
